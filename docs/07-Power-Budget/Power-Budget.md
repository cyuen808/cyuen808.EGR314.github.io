# Power Budget

## Overview

This power budget verifies that the selected regulators and battery source can safely power all major components in the Front Arm subsystem. Following the same format as the example template, a 25% safety margin was added to each rail to satisfy EGR 314 expectations.

### This updated version reflects the current Front Arm design using:
- **ESP32-S3-WROOM-1** as the controller
- **DRV8434S** as the stepper motor driver
- **LM22678** as the 5 V buck regulator
- **LM2651** as the 3.3 V regulator
- **+12 V Li-ion battery pack** as the main source 

---

## Section A – Major Power-Consuming Components

| Component | Voltage Rail | Max Current |
|---|---:|---:|
| ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 | 3.3 V | 0.50 A *(budgeted peak)* |
| DRV8434S logic / control | 3.3 V | 0.01 A *(budgeted)* |
| DRV8434S + Stepper Motor (VM rail) | +12 V | 2.50 A |
| 5 V auxiliary rail load | 5 V | 0.50 A *(estimated input to 3.3 V stage)* |

---

## Section B – Power Rail Totals (With 25% Safety Margin)

### 3.3 V Rail

Total current:  
`0.50 + 0.01 = 0.51 A`

With 25% margin:  
`0.51 × 1.25 = 0.638 A`

Selected regulator: **LM2651**  
Maximum output current: **1.5 A** 

---

### 5 V Rail

The 5 V rail is generated by the **LM22678** from the battery input. In this design, the 5 V rail mainly supports the downstream 3.3 V conversion stage and future auxiliary peripherals.

Estimated 5 V rail current:  
`0.50 A`

With 25% margin:  
`0.50 × 1.25 = 0.625 A`

Selected regulator: **LM22678**  
Maximum output current: **5 A** 
---

### 14.4 V Motor Rail

Motor / VM rail current:  
`2.50 A`

With 25% margin:  
`2.50 × 1.25 = 3.125 A`

Power source: **12 V Li-ion battery pack**  
Maximum continuous discharge current: **5.0 A** 

---

## Section C – Regulator / Source Selection

| Rail | Source / Regulator | Max Output | Required Current | Status |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 3.3 V | LM2651 | 1.5 A | 0.638 A | ✔ Safe |
| 5 V | LM22678 | 5.0 A | 0.625 A | ✔ Safe |

## Section D – External Power Source

Selected external power source: **GlobTek 4S Li-ion battery pack**

- **5000 mAh**
- **72 Wh**
- **5.0 A maximum continuous discharge current**
